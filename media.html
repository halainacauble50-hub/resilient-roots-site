Meet Halaina

Author • Speaker • Salon Owner

Halaina is the author of Resilient Roots, a memoir of resilience, faith, forgiveness, and hope. Born in Ramallah and now living in South Carolina, she writes with honesty and compassion, encouraging readers to discover strength in life's greatest challenges.

For more than twenty-two years, Halaina has owned and operated a successful salon where thousands of conversations have revealed the power of listening, healing, and human connection. Those experiences continue to inspire her books, interviews, and speaking engagements.

Whether appearing on television, speaking at bookstores, libraries, and book clubs, or connecting one-on-one with readers, Halaina's message remains the same: our past may shape us, but it does not have to define our future.
